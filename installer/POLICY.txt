AgentCo — what it does on this computer

Please read this before installing. It is short on purpose, and it is written
plainly rather than as a legal formula, because the point is that you actually
know what you are agreeing to.


WHAT AGENTCO DOES

AgentCo runs a team of AI assistants on this machine, using your own Claude
account. To do useful work, it can:

  * READ AND WRITE FILES in the folders you point it at. It creates documents,
    edits them, and deletes what it has written when you ask it to.

  * RUN COMMANDS that you declare yourself. You write the command; AgentCo runs
    it with the values an assistant fills in.

  * CONNECT TO SERVICES you sign in to — for example Notion, GitHub or Google
    Calendar — with the access level you choose when you connect them.

  * SEND WHAT YOU TYPE, and the contents of documents you give it, TO ANTHROPIC,
    because that is where Claude runs. Your usage is subject to your own
    agreement with Anthropic.


WHAT AGENTCO DOES NOT DO

  * It does not send your documents, results or company data anywhere except to
    Anthropic as part of answering you.

  * It does not have an account system, and it does not upload anything to us.
    We operate no server that holds your data.

  * It does not read outside the folders you have given it, and it does not run
    commands you have not declared.

  * It checks once a day whether a newer version exists. That request contains
    no identifier of you or your machine, and it can be switched off.


WHERE THE BOUNDARIES ARE

These are real limits built into the product, not promises:

  * Each office works inside its own folder. That folder is the working root.

  * A connection is granted to specific employees, not to the whole company.

  * Every call an assistant makes through a connection is written to an audit
    log you can read.

  * A command you declare can be marked read-only, and then it can only read.


YOUR RESPONSIBILITY

AgentCo does what you and your assistants tell it to do, on real files, with
real accounts, on this real computer. AI assistants make mistakes, and a mistake
here can mean a file changed or deleted, a message sent, or money spent on your
Claude account.

You are responsible for what you point it at and what you allow it to do.

Keep backups of anything you cannot afford to lose. Start with a folder where a
mistake would not matter. Read what an assistant proposes before letting it run
commands that change things.
